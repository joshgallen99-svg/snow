Controls

How to ride

Keyboard for riding, touchpad or mouse for the mountain cam and the orbit camera.

AD or ←→
Carve left and right. In the air: spin.
W / ↑
Tuck for speed. In the air: frontflip. On flats: skate.
S / ↓
Skid to slow down. In the air: backflip.
Space
Hold to crouch, release to ollie. Pop right at a kicker lip for extra air. Jump off rails.
JKLI
Grabs: Indy, Melon, Tail, Nose. Let go before you land.
C
Slow motion with an orbit camera. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
R
Respawn at your last safe spot.
E
Ride the lift you're standing at.
M
Open the mountain cam (trail map).
N
Music on or off.
Esc / P
Pause.
